We did not have any updates required by the customers, so there isn't anything to say there,
unfortunately.  When it comes to their design, in all honesty there are some parts of our design
that we became more grateful for as a learned lesson, that ours felt relatively easy to use and
lacking in unnecessary complexity after the changes we made between assignments.

The only thing we did realize was lacking in our design, mostly through the need of it for a proper
adaption, is the ability to get a copy of a player's remaining deck.  While this was never needed
at all for our view/controller implementation, it may be handy to have available as a publicly
exposed method, in case it were to be used for a more advanced view that has the number of deck
cards left remaining, or shows upcoming cards for the sake of planning.

Our providers were very helpful for communicating with.  We had made a pretty big ask of them early
on to submit us four new interfaces that were required for us to be able to create our adapter
properly, and they were able to do so.  Along with this, they listened well to make minor changes
to these interfaces regarding types, even if the request to do so was late into the assignment.
